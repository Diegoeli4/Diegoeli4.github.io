Multiple Literacies & Goal Setting

Course outcome: show how I use my different literacies (speaking, listening, reading, multilingual writing, translating, multimodality, and more) to support my writing process.

What This Outcome Means to Me

To me, literacy means more than just being able to read and write. Literacy includes the different ways I communicate, understand information, and express my ideas, such as speaking, listening, reading, writing, and using more than one language.

Context

My literacy narrative was a piece of writing where I reflected on my experiences with language and literacy. I wrote about being born in São Paulo, Brazil, living there until I was six, and then moving to the United States. The purpose of the assignment was to explain how my experiences with language and communication have shaped me as a writer. My audience was my classmates and instructor, so I wanted to explain my experiences in a way that others could understand while also showing how they affected my development.

Reflection

This artifact demonstrates how I use multiple literacies because my experiences with Portuguese and English have both influenced the way I communicate and write. Reading and writing in English have helped me become more comfortable with academic writing

Transfer

I can use these literacies in future classes and in my career. Since I am interested in computer science and cybersecurity, I will need to communicate technical information to people with different levels of knowledge. I may need to read technical documentation, write reports, explain problems to others, listen to teammates, and communicate clearly with people who are not familiar with technology.
