Welcome to my ePortfolio

A working collection of my writing, projects, and reflections on how I'm growing as a writer and a Computer Science student.

About Me

Hello, I'm Diego Goncalves Eliseu, a freshman studying Computer Science at UCF. I moved from Brazil to Florida when I was six, and I've lived here ever since. After graduation, I hope to work in software engineering or cybersecurity. This ePortfolio is where I share my work and reflect on how I'm growing as a writer in a technical field.

About This ePortfolio

This website is a semester-long project for ENC1101 at UCF. It is where I collect my best work, organize it, and write about what I learned while making it. You'll find drafts, finished pieces, and reflections, and the site will keep growing through my freshman year.

Purpose

I'm building this portfolio to show how my writing changes over time: how I plan, revise, respond to feedback, and write for different audiences. I also want it to outlast this class, so I'm designing it to hold coding projects too.
